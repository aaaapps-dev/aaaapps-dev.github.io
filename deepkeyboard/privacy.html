Privacy Policy – Deep Keyboard

App: Deep Keyboard (com.deepkeyboard.app)
Developer: AAAAPPS
Contact: aaaappsupport@gmail.com
Effective date: November 18, 2026

Deep Keyboard is a keyboard app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. We do not run our own servers for your data, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your personal information.

What you type

Deep Keyboard learns from what you type and paste so it can suggest your next words: words, sentences, emoji, e-mail addresses, links and numbers you use often. This learning happens only on your phone and is never sent to us. The keyboard does not learn from password fields. Numbers longer than 12 digits, such as card numbers, are never saved. You can remove any suggested word by long-pressing it in the prediction bar, and you can delete everything the keyboard learned at any time in Settings.

Clipboard, shortcuts, stickers and replies

Your clipboard history, text shortcuts, number key replies, the photos and videos you add to replies, the stickers you make and your collections are stored only on your phone, inside the app.

GIFs and stickers search

When you open or search GIFs and stickers, the app sends your search words, your device language and a random ID created by the app to KLIPY (klipy.com), the service that provides the GIFs and stickers. This random ID does not identify you or your device. Like any internet service, KLIPY also receives your IP address. No ads are shown. KLIPY handles this information under its own privacy policy, available on klipy.com.

Voice typing

When you use voice typing, the app uses your phone's microphone, and the speech recognition service on your phone (usually Google) turns your speech into text. The app does not keep or send your recordings to us. Voice typing works only while you use it.

Translator

Translation runs on your phone using Google ML Kit. Language packs are downloaded once from Google, and the text you translate stays on your phone.

Language dictionaries

Dictionaries for extra languages are downloaded from our public download page on GitHub (aaaapps-dev.github.io) when you add a language. Only the file is downloaded and nothing about you is sent.

Backup to Google Drive (optional)

If you turn on backup, you sign in with your Google account. Your learned words, settings, layouts, themes, shortcuts and replies are saved in a private app folder in your own Google Drive and come back when you sign in on another phone. Only Deep Keyboard can read this folder, and we have no access to it. The app reads your account e-mail only to show you which account is used. You can turn backup off at any time.

Updates and settings file

The app reads a small settings file from our public page on GitHub to know about new versions and which features are available. Nothing about you is sent.

Premium

Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Permissions

Microphone: only for voice typing, when you use it.
Internet: for GIFs and stickers, dictionaries, backup, the translator's language packs and updates.
Vibration: for key feedback.
User dictionary: to read and add words in your phone's personal dictionary.

Children

Deep Keyboard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

If you have any questions, contact AAAAPPS at aaaappsupport@gmail.com.
